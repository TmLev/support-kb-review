0002 · Deduplicate by evidence and failure path

owner TmLev proposed reviewed 2026-10-29 · 0 days ago

Context

Ticket #4471 combines distinct parser and booking-form hint failures, plus unresolved temporal observations. The subject-only rule in accepted decision 0001 hid these distinctions. Similar words do not prove a shared cause.

Proposed decision

Replace subject-only merging with a check of the body, tenant locale, form version and channel, followed by a reproduced failure path. Use the subject to find candidates, not to decide the merge. Deduplicate export rows by stable report ID. Hold uncertain reports separately and preserve their evidence. Record received time and merge time separately. Count from data in a component and attach report IDs to every issue, so another reviewer can reproduce the scope.

Consequences

Triage takes longer than matching subject text, but different fixes remain visible and new reports can update the right investigation. Keep decision 0001 unchanged as history. This proposal takes effect only when the desk accepts it; no approval is claimed here.

See the incident review for the rendered counts and concrete counterexamples.
